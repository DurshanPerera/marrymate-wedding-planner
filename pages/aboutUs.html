About MarryMate

Your Complete Wedding Planning Platform in Sri Lanka

MarryMate is a modern web-based platform designed to simplify wedding planning. We connect couples with trusted vendors, provide smart budgeting tools, and offer an all-in-one system to manage every aspect of a wedding — from discovery to booking.

100+Vendors
500+Users
200+Bookings

Our Mission

To make wedding planning simple, efficient, and stress-free by providing a centralized platform where users can discover vendors, manage budgets, and organize their entire wedding journey in one place.

Our Vision

To become Sri Lanka's leading digital wedding platform by transforming traditional wedding planning into a smart, technology-driven experience that benefits both customers and vendors.

What MarryMate Does

Powerful features to simplify your wedding planning journey

Smart Budget Planner

Automatically distributes the wedding budget across categories and suggests suitable vendors based on the user's preferences.

Vendor Discovery System

Explore both company and individual vendors with advanced filtering by category, location, and service type.

Booking Management

Send booking requests to vendors and track their status (pending, approved, rejected) directly from the dashboard.

Saved Vendors

Save your favorite vendors for quick access and future decision-making.

Messaging System

Communicate directly with vendors for inquiries and discussions.

Web Scraping Integration

Enhanced vendor listings with external vendor data, giving users a wider range of options.
